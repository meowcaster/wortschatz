# 🇩🇪 Wortschatz — a free German trainer

Learn German vocabulary from beginner (**A1**) to advanced (**C1**) — free, no account, no ads.

## ▶️ Open the app

### 👉 https://meowcaster.github.io/wortschatz/

Works on your **phone** and **computer**. Open the link, tap **Install**, and it adds itself to your home screen like a normal app — and then works **offline**.

## What's inside

- **12,000+ words**, A1 → C1, plus hand-picked themed decks — travel, food, health, work, opinions, and more
- **Spaced repetition** — shows each word right before you'd forget it, and paces new words (default 20/day) so you're never buried
- **🔊 Audio** — hear every word and example spoken aloud
- **Gender colour-coding** — der (blue) · die (red) · das (green), so the articles actually stick, with masculine / feminine / neuter / plural labels
- **Verb conjugation drills** — the tricky ones: du **nimmst**, er **hilft**, ich **weiß** …
- **Works offline**, and your progress is saved on your own device

## Installing it

Open the link and tap **Install** — the app detects your browser and shows the exact steps:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android (Chrome):** the **Install** prompt, or ⋮ menu → **Install app**
- **Desktop (Chrome / Edge):** the **install icon** at the right of the address bar

That's it — enjoy, and *viel Erfolg beim Lernen!* 🎉

---

<sub><b>For developers:</b> this is a static PWA — no build step, no tracking. The whole app is <code>index.html</code> (HTML + CSS + JS in one file), a frequency word list in <code>words.json</code>, a service worker (<code>sw.js</code>), and icons, served by GitHub Pages. To ship an update, edit the files and bump <code>VERSION</code> in <code>sw.js</code> so installed apps refresh.</sub>
